What we do

We answer plumbing calls around the clock and send licensed plumbers to homes and businesses in Mahwah, Ramsey, Oakland and Allendale. Services range from drain cleaning and water heater replacement to sewer line repair and sump pump service. See the full list of plumbing services.

We do not invent credentials or numbers. We do not show customer counts, star ratings or testimonials, because we would rather earn those honestly than print them. What we can promise is a clear price before work starts and a straight answer about what is possible the same day.

What we do not do

  • We do not pump or inspect septic tanks.
  • We do not service oil burners.
  • We do not repair structural foundations or exterior waterproofing membranes.
  • We do not promise arrival times. We offer same-day appointments when they are available and say so when they are not.

Checking any plumber in New Jersey

Under New Jersey law, plumbing may only be performed by a master plumber, an authorized plumbing contractor or employees of one. Ask any plumber for their license number and verify it with the New Jersey State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, part of the Division of Consumer Affairs.

The board's information is at njconsumeraffairs.gov.

New Jersey adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code through N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, so the same standard applies to every plumber working in Mahwah, Ramsey, Oakland and Allendale.

How we price work

Every job is different, so we do not print price lists. The plumber states the price before work starts, and you approve it. Factors that move the price are listed on each service page, so you know what to ask. If a plumber cannot or will not give a price before starting, treat that as a warning.

After-hours visits can carry a different rate. That rate is part of the price you hear before you agree.

Why we publish the limits

A plumbing line that says yes to everything ends up breaking promises. We list what we do not handle so you can call the right trade first and save the time. If your problem sits outside our work, we will say so on the call and, where we can, point you to the kind of specialist who handles it.

We also tell you when a repair is not worth making. An old fixture that needs its third part in a year may cost less to replace, and the plumber will explain the choice in plain words before you decide.
