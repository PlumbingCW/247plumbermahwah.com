Oakland and the Ramapo River

The Ramapo River flows south out of Mahwah and runs through West Oakland. Oakland is a borough of 8.77 square miles and 12,748 residents at the 2020 census, with Ramapo Valley Road, which is U.S. Route 202, running through its downtown. Homes near the river and on the low ground around Ramapo Lake have the highest exposure when water rises.

Parks, hills and wooded lots

Oakland borders the Ramapo Mountain State Forest, Great Oak Park, Ramapo Lake and the former Camp Tamarack. Many homes sit on wooded lots and hillsides, where roots reach sewer laterals and runoff finds foundations. A property on a slope above a stream often sheds water toward the house during storms, so downspouts, grading and a working sump pump all matter.

  • Roots in older laterals cause repeat backups that a sewer camera inspection explains.
  • Hillside runoff against a foundation points to drainage work, covered under basement waterproofing.
  • Homes on private wells or septic, where they exist, are outside our scope. We handle house-side plumbing and sewer lines.

Oakland water and your plumbing

Veolia holds a municipal consent from the borough to provide water service in Oakland, and the New Jersey Board of Public Utilities approved an expansion of that territory in 2023. If you have questions about water quality or hardness, ask Veolia for its annual water quality report. Scale on fixtures and rumbling in a water heater tell you how hard your own water is, and a flush of the heater is a good first step.
