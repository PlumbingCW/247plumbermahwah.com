Sewer Camera Inspection in Mahwah, NJ

A sewer camera inspection puts a waterproof camera on a flexible cable and feeds it through your drain line, so you can see the pipe wall, the joints and whatever is blocking the flow. Sewer camera inspection in Mahwah, NJ is the fastest way to learn why a line keeps clogging, whether a home you are buying has a hidden problem and what repair, if any, the line needs. What a camera inspection shows

A camera sees what a cable cannot. It shows roots reaching through a joint, a belly where water sits, cracks, offsets, scale and grease. It also shows what is not wrong, which is just as useful when you are deciding whether to spend money on a repair.

What the footage can confirm

  • ✓The pipe material, whether cast iron, clay, fiber, PVC or something else
  • ✓Roots, grease, scale and objects blocking the line
  • ✓Cracks, breaks, collapses and joints that have pulled apart
  • ✓Bellies or sags where water collects
  • ✓Connections from other lines and how the main joins the street sewer
  • ✓The distance from the cleanout to each problem, noted on the video

How the inspection works

  1. Find access

    The camera goes in through the main cleanout, a toilet flange or a vent pipe if no cleanout exists.

  2. Run the line

    The operator advances the camera and watches the screen, stopping at joints, bends and any trouble spot.

  3. Locate problems on the surface

    A transmitter in the camera head sends a signal so the plumber can mark the depth and location above ground.

  4. Record and review

    The video is saved, and the plumber talks you through it on the spot.

  5. Recommend next steps

    A clean line may need no work. A problem line gets options with trade-offs, and nothing is done without your approval.

Inspections for home buyers in Mahwah

Mahwah has older housing stock and newer developments, and the sewer lines in older homes can predate PVC. A pre-purchase camera check can reveal a root-filled joint or a failing cast iron stack that a home inspector will not see. If the report is clean, you buy with more confidence, and if it is not, you have a basis to ask for a repair credit.

Check whether the home connects to a township sewer or has a private septic system. We inspect house-side drain and sewer lines and do not pump or inspect septic tanks themselves.

Reading the results

Light roots or scale

Often handled with cleaning and then monitored on a schedule. See sewer line cleaning.

Cracks or offsets

May call for a spot repair or lining. See sewer line repair.

Bellies and sags

Cleaning only helps for a while. Replacement of the sagging section is the lasting answer.

A clean, sound pipe

No work is needed, and the video is a baseline for the future.

Codes and standards that apply

A camera report is most useful when it lists the footage, the location of each defect and the pipe material. New Jersey adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code through N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, and that code governs any repair that follows. Ask for the recording and the written findings.
