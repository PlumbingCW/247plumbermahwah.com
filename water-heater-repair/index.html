When a water heater problem is an emergency

Cold water is an inconvenience. Water on the floor is an emergency. A leaking tank can empty a full tank into a finished basement in an hour or two, and a relief valve that discharges steadily means the heater is under pressure it cannot shed. Gas models add a second risk, which is any smell of gas near the unit.

What to do first

  • !Smell of gas: leave the house, then call your gas utility or 911. Do not flip switches on the way out
  • !Water leaking from the tank body: close the cold water valve on top of the heater and shut off the gas control or the breaker
  • !Hot water or steam from the relief valve pipe: turn off the heater and keep people away from the discharge
  • !Rust-colored hot water: schedule a repair visit, since it often means a corroding tank or anode

After-hours repair calls are taken the same way as daytime calls. Describe what you see and we will tell you whether the unit can be repaired or whether the tank has failed.

Hard water and your tank

Mahwah's 2026 water quality report lists hardness from 91 to 203 ppm as calcium carbonate. In that range, minerals settle out of heated water and build up on the bottom of a tank or on electric heating elements. The sediment insulates the water from the burner or element, which makes the heater work harder and often produces the rumbling many homeowners hear.

An annual flush removes much of that sediment. A tank that has never been flushed may have a drain valve clogged with scale, so the first flush is sometimes a repair job in itself.

Repair or replace: a decision framework

Repair makes sense when

The tank is dry, the problem is a part such as an element, thermostat, igniter or valve, and the unit is not near the end of its life.

Replace when

The tank leaks, the unit is well past a decade old, or the repair would cost a large share of a new heater.

Ask about

The age on the data plate, whether the anode rod can still be serviced and what the warranty covers.

Never skip

A working relief valve and a discharge pipe that runs safely to a drain.

If the diagnosis points to a failed tank, the next step is water heater replacement, and planned upgrades such as a tankless unit can be priced at the same visit.

What affects the price of a water heater repair

A thermocouple and a gas control valve are different jobs, so a single price cannot describe repair work. The plumber should give you a number before starting. These factors decide it.

Homeowners insurance generally covers sudden water damage from a failed heater but not the heater itself. Wear, corrosion and long-term leaks are typically excluded, so read your policy.

Codes and standards that apply

Repairs to a water heater follow the manufacturer's installation standards for the model, and New Jersey adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code through N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15 for the piping and relief valve discharge. A part replaced with the wrong rating can void the warranty. Ask the plumber which part number and rating they are using.
