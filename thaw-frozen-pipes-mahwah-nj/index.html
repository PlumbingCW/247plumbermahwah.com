How to Thaw Frozen Pipes: Mahwah, NJ Winter Guide

When a faucet runs dry on a freezing morning, a pipe may be frozen. A frozen pipe is not yet a burst pipe, and the next few minutes decide which it becomes. This guide covers what to do, what never to do and how to keep it from happening again.

If you see water spraying or dripping from a pipe, close the main water valve first and call. That is no longer a frozen pipe.

Signs a pipe is frozen

  • A faucet that gives a trickle or nothing in freezing weather.
  • Frost on a visible pipe, or a pipe that feels very cold compared with others.
  • Strange noises, such as banging or gurgling, from pipes in an outside wall.
  • A smell from a drain, where a frozen vent or trap has stopped the airflow.

Pipes freeze first where cold air reaches them. Exterior walls, garages, crawlspaces, attics and unheated rooms are the usual places, along with outdoor spigots and any pipe near a vent or a gap in the insulation.

Thaw it safely

  1. Open the faucet

    Leave the faucet on so that water can flow as soon as the ice melts, and so the melting relieves pressure.

  2. Find the frozen section

    Follow the pipe from the faucet toward the cold area. The frozen part is the coldest and may have frost.

  3. Apply gentle heat

    Use a hair dryer on low, an electric heating pad or warm towels. Work from the faucet end toward the frozen section.

  4. Be patient

    It can take a while. Keep applying heat until water flows at full pressure.

  5. Check for leaks

    When water returns, inspect the pipe for cracks or drips along its length.

What never to do

  • !Never use a propane torch, a blowtorch or any open flame. It can start a fire and burst the pipe
  • !Never use a kerosene or propane heater in a closed space
  • !Do not thaw a pipe you cannot reach or see
  • !Do not leave a heat source unattended on a pipe or near insulation
  • !Do not turn the water on at full pressure before the faucet is open

When to shut off the main and call

If the pipe is behind a wall, under a slab or you cannot find the frozen section, shut off the main water valve and call a plumber. A pipe that has frozen may have split without leaking, because the ice is plugging the crack. When it thaws, the water pours through. That is why a thawed pipe should be inspected.

See our page on burst pipe repair if a pipe has split, and leak detection for slow leaks that appear after a freeze.

If you are leaving for the winter

An empty house in January is the setting for the worst burst pipe stories, because nobody is there to notice. If you will be away, shut off the main water valve and drain the system by opening the lowest faucet and the highest faucet. Leave the heat on at a safe temperature as well.

  • Ask a neighbor or a family member to check the house, and give them the plumber's number.
  • Open cabinet doors on exterior walls so room air reaches the pipes.
  • Leave the water heater on or drain it, depending on the manual, and never leave it running on an empty tank.
  • Tell your insurer if the house will be vacant for a long time, since some policies limit coverage.

Know where the main shutoff is before the first hard freeze. Tag the valve, test that it turns fully and keep a flashlight nearby. If a pipe does burst, closing that valve within minutes limits the damage, and a plumber can then repair the break in a dry house.

Mahwah water service notes

Mahwah's water system is run by the township, and its Department of Public Works Water and Sewer office handles it. If your water is out and your neighbors' water is also out, the cause may be on the main and not in your house. Call the township first in that case.

About two thirds of Mahwah's water comes from seven wells along the Ramapo River valley, and the rest is supplied by Veolia, according to the township's 2026 water quality report. Wells and a municipal system still deliver to individual homes through service lines that can freeze when shallow. See water service line repair.

We respond to freezing-weather calls in Mahwah and in the nearby boroughs of Ramsey and Oakland.
