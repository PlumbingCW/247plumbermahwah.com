About Cragmere Park

Mahwah Township describes Cragmere Park as a long-established section that is residential only. The Mahwah Museum records that the land came from the estate of Colonel Ezra Miller, and that The Mahwah Company began selling lots on June 1, 1909. The company sold lots but did not build the houses.

Deed restrictions ruled out business, manufacturing, apartments and two-family houses, and required the company to approve house plans. The Cragmere Association formed in December 1911 and took over the streets, the lake and the park in 1912. That planned, residential-only layout is why the section still feels like a single neighborhood.

Streets, slopes and the lake

The museum says the streets follow the natural contours of the land. Airmount Road and Mahwah Road run east to west and are steep. Oweno Lake and its park were set aside as the central green space. Other streets named in the record include Maysenger, Alcott, Armour, Beveridge, Malcolm, Highland and Summit Roads.

Slope matters to a plumber in general ways. Water pressure can differ between a house at the top of a hill and one at the bottom, and drain lines run with gravity toward the street or the main. This is general plumbing information, not a finding about any one house, so a plumber checks the actual pressure and the actual drain route on site.

Houses from the 1910s, with additions over time

The museum reports that many of the first houses were built in the 1910s, with later additions to some. The first house, at 103 Oweno Road, was nearly complete in November 1909. Notable early homes include one on Airmount Road built in 1912. We do not know the pipe material in any particular house, and house age alone does not tell us.

What a long history does mean is that many homes have had work done in more than one era. A kitchen added in one decade and a bathroom in another can leave supply lines, drains and fittings from different periods in the same house. Ask the plumber to look at where each line enters the house and to say what they see before recommending any change.

Water in Cragmere Park

The museum says Cragmere had its own early water system and reservoir at the bend of Mahwah Road. The system was sold in 1925 and became part of Mahwah's water system in 1950. Today the township's Water and Sewer office handles service and billing questions, so call it if you are unsure about your street.

The 2026 Mahwah water quality report, covering 2025, gives hardness as 91 to 203 parts per million. On the USGS scale that runs from moderately hard to very hard. Calcium in that report is 52 parts per million, and the pH range is 6.8 to 8.2. Scale on fixtures and a rumbling water heater are signs of mineral buildup in the home, whatever the street.

When to call right away

Treat these as urgent

  • !Water or sewage coming up through a tub, toilet or floor drain

  • !A leaking water heater tank on a finished floor
  • !A smell of gas, in which case leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first

With a leak, close the main water valve and then call. With a backup, stop running water down the drains until a plumber has looked.
