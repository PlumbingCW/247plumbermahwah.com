What to have ready when you call

Your address

Include the town and a nearby cross street if your house is hard to find.

What you see

Where the water is, how much and whether it is clear, brown or smells.

What you tried

Valves you closed, plungers or cleaners you used and when the problem began.

Equipment details

The make and size of a water heater or the type of faucet, if you can read the label.

While you wait for a plumber

Steps that limit damage

  • ✓Close the main water valve for any leak you cannot stop at a fixture
  • ✓Stop using drains if water is backing up
  • ✓Turn off power to wet areas only if you can do so without standing in water
  • ✓Photograph damage before cleanup, since insurers will ask
  • ✓If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 first
