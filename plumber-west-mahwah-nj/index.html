About West Mahwah

Mahwah Township lists West Mahwah as a section that is mostly residential, with some businesses and industries. Other sources describe it as a mixed residential and commercial area. The township does not publish its boundaries on that page, so we describe it by an anchor street. West Ramapo Avenue is the road the Mahwah Museum names at the north end of the old Valley Road improvements.

The Valley Road is now Route 202, and the museum says it was improved from Darlington Avenue north to West Ramapo Avenue. We use these roads only as anchors. If you are unsure whether your street counts as West Mahwah, call and tell us the address.

Homes and small businesses on the same call line

Township records show where the parcels in Mahwah fall. Across the whole township there are 9,196 residential parcels, against 66 commercial and 66 office and industrial parcels. Those are township-wide figures, not West Mahwah figures. They do show that homes dominate, and that a business is the exception.

That mix means two kinds of calls. A homeowner might need a water heater or a slow drain handled. A small shop or office might have a restroom that backs up or a hot water system that fails. The first questions are the same: where is the problem, how fast is it getting worse, and where is the main shutoff?

Small business calls

For a shop, an office or a light industrial unit, see our commercial plumbing page. A tenant should check the lease, because the landlord may be responsible for some lines and the tenant for others. Tell the landlord quickly when water might reach another unit.

  • Know where the building's main shutoff is, and tell staff.
  • Keep a contact for the property manager near the phone.
  • Photograph damage and the time it began, for the landlord and the insurer.
  • Stop using a backing-up fixture until it is checked.

Water in West Mahwah

The township Water and Sewer office can tell you whether your street is on the municipal system. Its water quality report puts the well share at 67.2 percent, drawn from seven wells in the Ramapo River valley aquifer, with the other 32.8 percent coming from Veolia.

A business with a water heater or a dishwasher feels hard water the same way a house does. The township report lists hardness of 91 to 203 parts per million, and a plumber can test your own tap if scale is building up.

Nearby sections

Other sections of the township with their own pages are Cragmere Park and Fardale. Our Mahwah emergency plumber page covers the whole township.
