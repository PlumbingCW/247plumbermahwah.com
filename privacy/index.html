Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you call us or visit this website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

This is a call-only service. When you call, we collect your phone number, your address, your name if you give it and a description of the problem. We use it to send a plumber, to confirm the price and to follow up on the job.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell us at the start of the call.

This website

The website has no forms and does not ask for personal information. Like most websites, it may receive standard technical information from your browser, such as the page requested. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and may show a Google map. Those third parties receive your browser's request and handle it under their own policies.

How we use information

  • To dispatch a plumber and confirm the visit.
  • To give you the price and to bill for the work.
  • To follow up on the job and answer questions.
  • To meet legal and insurance requirements.

Sharing

We share your name, address and the details of the problem with the plumber assigned to your job. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you, subject to legal and business record requirements. Email contact@247plumbermahwah.com or call (551) 249-3045.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
