How to choose the right service

Plumbing problems tend to fall into four groups. Water coming out of a pipe, water not leaving a drain, hot water that is not hot and water coming into the basement. The first group is leaks and bursts, the second is drains and sewers, the third is heaters and boilers and the fourth is sump pumps and drainage.

Some work is handled inside a related page and not on a page of its own. Frozen pipe repair is covered under burst pipe repair. Water meter and high-bill investigations and slab leak detection are covered under leak detection. Backflow testing, grease trap cleaning and storm drain cleaning are covered under commercial plumbing. Cast iron pipe repair sits inside sewer line repair, and hybrid and gas water heaters sit inside water heater repair.

Emergency plumber service in general, the 24-hour and same-day line, is on the homepage.

What every service page includes

The standards we point to are public. New Jersey adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code through N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, and each page tells you to ask the local construction office about permits.

Each page opens with what to do while you wait, then explains the causes, the methods and the repair options in plain language. A price section lists the factors that move the cost, so you know what to ask. A short local section ties the advice to Mahwah, Ramsey, Oakland or Allendale.

We do not print prices, ratings or customer counts. We would rather give you the information to judge a quote than a number that may not fit your house.
