What to do first in a plumbing emergency

The first few minutes decide how much damage a plumbing emergency does. Water that runs for an hour soaks floors, ceilings and everything stored in a basement. A sewer backup is a health problem as well. These steps work for most emergencies, and then the call to a plumber comes next.

Before the plumber arrives

  • !Burst or leaking pipe: close the main water valve, then open a low faucet to drain the lines
  • !Sewer backup: stop using water, keep people and pets out of the area and do not use chemical cleaners
  • !Water heater leak: close the cold water valve above the tank and turn off the gas control or the breaker
  • !Smell of gas: leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 before calling a plumber
  • !Sump pit filling with no pump running: check the power, lift the float by hand and move belongings off the floor

Mahwah neighborhoods we serve

Mahwah is the largest municipality in Bergen County by area, and its character changes from one corner to the next. We take calls from all of it. Local knowledge helps when a house sits near the river, on a hill or in an older neighborhood where the pipes predate current materials.

Darlington, in the western valley, is home to the Crocker-McMillin Mansion and Darlington County Park. The Ramapo River runs close by, and basements in the valley are exposed to rising groundwater, so sump pumps and backups matter here. See sump pump repair.

Cragmere Park has historic homes documented by the Mahwah Museum. Older houses carry older pipes, and a sewer camera inspection or a look at the supply lines can show what needs attention before a failure.

West Mahwah and Masonicus mix older lots with newer building. Wooded lots near Campgaw Mountain Reservation and Ramapo Valley County Reservation mean roots, and roots mean repeat sewer backups. See drain cleaning and hydro jetting.

The Route 17 and I-287 corridor holds shops, restaurants and offices, and Ramapo College sits in the township as well. Businesses on that corridor need after-hours answers, covered under commercial plumbing. We also take calls from Ringwood on the northwest edge of the area.

What Mahwah water does to plumbing

Mahwah's 2026 water quality report says about 67 percent of the water comes from seven wells along the Ramapo River Valley aquifer and the rest is supplied by Veolia. Hardness runs from 91 to 203 ppm as calcium carbonate, which spans moderately hard to very hard on the USGS scale. In practice that means scale in water heaters, tankless heat exchangers, faucet aerators and old drain lines.

The same report says the township adds a phosphate compound that forms a protective coating inside pipes to reduce lead release. Flushing the water heater each year is the simplest habit that pays off. See water heater repair and tankless water heater service.

The river, flood stage and your basement

The Ramapo River at Mahwah has a National Weather Service flood stage of 8 feet. The highest recorded crest in its historical list is 15.77 feet, set in August 2011. Not every home is near the water, but a high water table pushes into basements well away from the bank, and storms often take the power with them.

Choosing a plumber in New Jersey

New Jersey adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code through N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, and that code sets the standards that licensed plumbers work to. Permit rules vary by town, so ask the local construction office before major work.

New Jersey law says plumbing may only be performed by a master plumber, an authorized plumbing contractor or their employees. Any plumber you hire should be able to tell you their license number, and you can check it with the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, part of the Division of Consumer Affairs. Ask for the price first, and be cautious of anyone who will not give one. See about us for how we work.
